# Saturn perpetual sUSDat tranching V3 — product specification

Status: frozen local v0 product specification for the unified V3 release. It does not authorize deployment, activation, a proxy change, or production parameters beyond the frozen review candidate. Launch `alpha`, exact role addresses, seizure requirements, and legal/accounting approval remain open.

## Judgment

The implemented V3 candidate tranches canonical multi-asset sUSDat as one balance sheet with two independently supplied fungible share classes:

- Senior receives a fixed participation `alpha` in eligible income generated by all approved sUSDat assets.
- Junior receives residual eligible income, ordinary portfolio appreciation and other non-eligible return, and absorbs first loss.
- Senior and Junior may enter or exit independently when the market is healthy and the post-operation coverage constraints hold.
- A proportional full-stack basket exit remains available whenever sUSDat transfers safely, without relying on live tranche NAV.

This supersedes the earlier paired-only wrapper mechanics—equal supplies, mandatory paired creation, equal-token recombination, and pair-only realization—while retaining the eligible-income accounting foundation as part of the unified V3 design.

## Confirmed direction

- Collateral is canonical sUSDat, including its approved multi-asset portfolio.
- There is one Senior class and one Junior class, not one class per underlying asset.
- Senior participates in portfolio-wide **eligible income**, not every positive movement in sUSDat NAV.
- Junior owns the portfolio-wide residual and first loss.
- Eligible Senior income is capitalized into Senior NAV; it is not paid as cash or a claimable reward in v0.
- Senior participation is fixed and immutable per deployment. The numerical `alpha` remains open.
- Each deployment has an immutable backing-value cap. V0 freezes the first-pilot ceiling at $500,000 equivalent of sUSDat.
- Preferred coverage is 150%, opening capitalization is exactly 2:1 Senior:Junior by value, and protocol fees are zero.
- The product is perpetual. No maturity, routine cash distribution, arrears, target clock, sunset, or final-settlement machinery is included in v0.
- If Junior NAV reaches zero, v0 does not recapitalize through the existing Junior token. New Junior deposits and impaired Senior redemptions close; organic recovery may revive the existing Junior.

## Product identity

The product is a funded, perpetual tranche of sUSDat:

\[
A = H + R
\]

where:

- `A` is the current value of sUSDat backing;
- `Q` is the contractual Senior claim;
- `H = min(A,Q)` is marked Senior NAV; and
- `R = A-H` is Junior residual NAV.

The vault issues two ERC-20 share classes against the same balance sheet. It must not be represented as two independent ERC-4626 vaults each owning the collateral.

Per-share accounting NAV is:

\[
P_S = H/S_{total}, \qquad P_J = R/J_{total}
\]

Accounting NAV is not a guaranteed price. Secondary markets remain market-priced.

## Return allocation

For each approved multiplier-based sUSDat asset, an asset-specific adapter identifies normalized cumulative eligible income. Actual funded USDat surplus is a separate cash-source pattern: it becomes eligible only as it is recognized under sUSDat's existing vesting policy. The vault aggregates both source patterns into one cumulative eligible-income-per-sUSDat-share index.

USDat is non-rebasing, but its backing may earn yield routed to Saturn's configured recipient. V0 books no projected backing yield or treasury receivable. Only surplus actually transferred into sUSDat and recognized through vesting enters Senior income. Ordinary user deposits, unvested surplus, and unsupported assets contribute zero eligible Senior income.

For portfolio eligible-income value `I`:

\[
\Delta Q = \alpha I
\]

- `alpha I` increases the Senior contractual claim.
- `(1-alpha)I` remains Junior residual.
- Ordinary asset appreciation, cash drag, fees, execution effects, and other non-eligible sUSDat return change `A` but not `Q`.
- Portfolio losses reduce Junior first. Senior is impaired only when `A < Q`.
- Recovery in `A` restores impaired Senior before creating new Junior residual.

Capitalized Senior income remains invested in sUSDat and is realized through healthy Senior redemption or a secondary-market sale. It creates no coupon, guaranteed APY, arrears, or protocol debt.

## Normal actions

### Senior deposit

A healthy deposit adds equal value to backing and the Senior contractual account:

\[
A' = A+x, \qquad Q'=Q+x
\]

Senior shares mint at current Senior NAV. The operation is limited by available Junior coverage.

### Senior redemption

A healthy redemption removes equal value from backing and the Senior contractual account:

\[
A'=A-x, \qquad Q'=Q-x
\]

Junior residual is unchanged and coverage improves. Senior redemption is closed during impairment in v0.

### Junior deposit

Junior capital increases backing and residual:

\[
A'=A+x, \qquad R'=R+x
\]

Junior shares mint at current Junior NAV. If Junior NAV is zero while old Junior supply exists, new deposits are closed in v0.

### Junior redemption

Junior may remove only coverage in excess of the preferred operating threshold:

\[
X_{J,max}=\max(0,A-C_pQ)
\]

The redemption burns Junior at current Junior NAV and must not reduce post-operation coverage below `C_p`.

### Senior-deposit capacity

New Senior may use existing excess Junior coverage:

\[
X_{S,max}=\max\left(0,\frac{A-C_pQ}{C_p-1}\right)
\]

If neither Senior deposits nor Junior redeems excess coverage, the excess remains invested and continues earning portfolio returns.

### Proportional full-stack exit

A holder burning the same fraction `p` of both outstanding classes receives fraction `p` of actual sUSDat custody:

\[
s/S_{total}=j/J_{total}=p
\]

This is the algebraic safety exit. It does not require equal token quantities or live oracle reads. Conservative rounding may leave bounded dust for remaining holders; it must never overpay the exiting holder.

## Coverage terminology

V3 uses two distinct economic concepts:

1. **Opening capitalization**: the initial Senior/Junior funding ratio.
2. **Preferred operating coverage `C_p`**: the threshold at which Senior-deposit and Junior-redemption capacity becomes zero.

A 70/30 opening stack gives `100/70 = 142.86%` Senior asset coverage. V0 instead initializes at an exact 2:1 Senior:Junior value ratio. Conservative integer rounding must leave opening coverage at or above 150%.

At exactly `A=C_pQ`, Senior-deposit and Junior-redemption capacity are both zero. Capacity opens only when `A>C_pQ`.

Actual impairment is defined directly as backing NAV below the contractual Senior claim. V0 does not add a second numerical hard-coverage threshold unless review identifies a concrete operation that needs it. A separate hard pause remains available for unsafe transfers, exploit containment, custody failure, or compliance enforcement; it is not an economic coverage ratio.

Worked examples remain illustrative; the frozen values are the explicit decisions above.

## Liveness and user promises

One-sided deposits and redemptions require:

- fresh, coherent sUSDat NAV;
- healthy approved income sources;
- settled portfolio eligible-income accounting;
- safe custody and lifecycle state;
- caller bounds and deadline; and
- atomic post-operation coverage enforcement.

They fail closed during stale, paused, deviating, or unresolved states.

Ordinary Senior and Junior transfers remain available. Proportional full-stack exit remains available whenever sUSDat itself can transfer safely. The core does not promise continuous one-sided liquidity or a secondary-market price equal to accounting NAV.

## Explicit v0 exclusions

- no fixed or guaranteed Senior APY;
- no promised Junior APY;
- no cash or claimable sUSDat distributions;
- no routine keeper or dividend checkpoint;
- no per-holder reward accounting;
- no separate asset-specific Senior classes;
- no maturity or recurring series;
- no Junior recapitalization epoch machinery;
- no impaired Senior redemption;
- no emergency final settlement or sunset machinery;
- no protocol-funded liquidity guarantee;
- no runtime collateral registry or governance-switched collateral;
- no arbitrary baskets.

## Product disclosures

- Senior participates only in approved portfolio eligible income; it does not receive ordinary portfolio appreciation merely because sUSDat NAV rises.
- Approved portfolio eligible income includes actual funded USDat surplus as it becomes recognized through vesting; merely accrued backing yield and unvested surplus are not counted.
- Junior receives residual income and non-eligible return but bears first loss across the complete sUSDat portfolio.
- Capitalized income increases Senior accounting NAV and remains exposed to the sUSDat balance sheet until redeemed.
- Senior and Junior accounting NAVs are not guaranteed market prices.
- One-sided protocol actions depend on healthy accounting and available coverage.
- sUSDat upgrades, composition, fees, execution, redemptions, oracle liveness, and asset migrations remain underlying dependencies.

## Review and launch sequence

1. Preserve the completed V3 STRCon and recognized-funded-USDat ledger integration.
2. Preserve the fixed-source boundary; do not generalize production storage until another asset is actually integrated into sUSDat.
3. Treat the implemented V3 accountant and class-token candidate as the review baseline; do not add product surface.
4. Obtain independent review and audit of the V3 eligible-income system and tranche layer together.
5. Run post-V2 live shadow reconciliation, then freeze launch `alpha` and the exact deployment manifest.

The implemented interface uses a non-upgradeable, versioned tranche accountant with immutable economic parameters and a proportional exit from old versions. The underlying sUSDat proxy remains a separate upgradeable dependency.

The backing cap is enforced on new Senior and Junior inflows using the same fresh sUSDat NAV required for one-sided accounting. Organic NAV growth or unsolicited transfers can move custody above the cap, but cannot mint claims; further inflows remain closed while healthy exits and proportional full-stack exit remain available.
